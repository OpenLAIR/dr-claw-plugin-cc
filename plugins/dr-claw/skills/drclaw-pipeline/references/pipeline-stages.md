# Pipeline Stages Reference

## Survey
Goal: Understand the research landscape before committing to a direction.
Key outputs: Literature review reports, gap analysis, baseline mapping.
Skills: inno-deep-research, academic-researcher, dataset-discovery, gemini-deep-research

## Ideation
Goal: Generate and evaluate research ideas, prepare resources.
Key outputs: Ranked ideas, selected idea, resource manifest.
Skills: inno-idea-generation, inno-idea-eval, inno-prepare-resources, research-ideation

## Experiment
Goal: Implement and validate the research idea.
Key outputs: Code, trained models, results, analysis figures/tables.
Skills: inno-code-survey, inno-experiment-dev, inno-experiment-analysis

## Publication
Goal: Write and review the paper.
Key outputs: Paper draft, figures, review report, reference audit.
Skills: inno-paper-writing, inno-figure-gen, inno-paper-reviewer, inno-reference-audit, inno-humanizer

## Promotion
Goal: Create materials to share the work.
Key outputs: Slide deck, narration audio, demo video.
Skills: making-academic-presentations
